Best way to use this explorer

Start with Strong athletic fit, then compare the school’s admissions difficulty and aid policy. For schools such as Amherst, Middlebury, Vassar and Hamilton, the volleyball fit can be much better than the admissions odds suggest. For elite programs such as MIT or Johns Hopkins, the opposite may be true: the school belongs on the aspirational list even when the volleyball level or current setter profile makes it a reach.

Methodology

Qualitative recruiting-fit assessment for Sara, not a statistical probability model. It combines (1) her profile — 1.71 m setter, Romanian U17 national champion, Best Setter U17, U19 experience and strong academics; (2) evidence from current rosters, especially setter heights and whether the program has recently recruited setters in her height range; (3) recent team strength and conference performance, because stronger teams are generally harder to break into; and (4) practical program context. ‘Strong athletic fit’ means her profile looks clearly plausible for that volleyball program; ‘Good athletic fit’ means the fit is credible but more competitive; ‘Athletic reach’ means she should still email the school, but the volleyball level or roster context makes it materially tougher.

What the chart encodings mean

  • Color = athletic fit: blue Strong, orange Good, green Reach.
  • Shape = division: circles are NCAA D1 and triangles NCAA D3.
  • Admissions reach is kept separate from volleyball fit. A school can be a Strong athletic fit and still an Extreme admissions reach.
  • Academic rank is the 2026 U.S. News position in the institution’s applicable category. The school universe was curated using the 2024–26 academic screen discussed in the research; always re-check rankings before final application decisions.
  • Volleyball strength is a project-specific 0–100 proxy based primarily on recent conference results and program level. It is not an official NCAA metric.

Data quality labels

  • Current official setter evidence comes from an official current/2026 roster page researched in this project.
  • Prior research reflects roster information found earlier and should be refreshed before outreach.
  • Not researched means no conclusion should be drawn. Missing aid or setter evidence is not evidence of absence.
Recruiting changes quickly. Coaches, rosters, aid policies and admissions practices change. The coach section includes a live refresh button that asks the existing CRA coach-finder service to re-check the official athletics site before you email.
